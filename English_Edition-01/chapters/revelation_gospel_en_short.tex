\textbf{(1) You Were Made by God, and You Are Precious to Him}

Your life is no accident. God made you in His own image, with a dignity no one can take away. You were made to know Him.
\begin{ScriptureBox}
\item[4:11] You are worthy, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power, for you created all things, and by your will they were created and have their being.
\end{ScriptureBox}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{(2) Jesus Loves You and Gave His Life to Redeem You}

Before Revelation speaks of judgment, it shows us a Savior. Jesus, the Lamb of God, gave His life and with His own blood bought people from every nation for God---including you.
\begin{ScriptureBox}
\item[1:5] To him who loves us and has freed us from our sins by his blood.
\item[5:9] Because you were slain, and with your blood you purchased for God persons from every tribe and language and people and nation.
\end{ScriptureBox}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{(3) Why We Need Him}

Each of us has wandered from God. We live as though we belong to ourselves, giving good things the place that belongs to God alone, blind to how poor we have become. One day every life will be laid open before Him, and no good deeds can wash away our guilt. Yet the Lord tells us this not to crush us, but because He loves us and longs for us to return.
\begin{ScriptureBox}
\item[3:17] You say, `I am rich; I have acquired wealth and do not need a thing.' But you do not realize that you are wretched, pitiful, poor, blind and naked.
\item[3:19] Those whom I love I rebuke and discipline. So be earnest and repent.
\item[20:12] The dead were judged according to what they had done as recorded in the books.
\end{ScriptureBox}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, labelsep=0.3em]
\item[Q.] \AskItem{Have you sensed that something is missing between you and God, and that you need His forgiveness?}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{(4) Come to Him Today}

\begin{ScriptureBox}
\item[7:14] They have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb.
\item[3:20] Here I am! I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in and eat with that person, and they with me.
\item[22:17] Let the one who is thirsty come; and let the one who wishes take the free gift of the water of life.
\item[21:4] He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the old order of things has passed away.
\end{ScriptureBox}

Those who stand before God are not those who never sinned, but those washed clean in the blood of the Lamb. You need not make yourself good enough first. Christ is knocking now. Come as you are: confess your sins, turn to Him, and believe that Jesus died and rose again for you. Salvation is His free gift.

\vspace{0.3cm}

Will you accept God's invitation today? You may pray in your own words, or pray this:

\hspace{0.5cm} In Jesus' name I pray. Amen.
