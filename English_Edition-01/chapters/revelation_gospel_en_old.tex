\textbf{(1) God Is the Creator of All Things and Alone Is Worthy of Worship}

Your life has a purpose. You were created in the image of God, endowed with a dignity that comes from Him. You are not a life without meaning or a being without a Creator. God made all things. He is the source of life and the Lord whom all humanity is called to worship.

\begin{ScriptureBox}
\item[4:11] You are worthy, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power, for you created all things, and by your will they were created and have their being.
\end{ScriptureBox}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, labelsep=0.3em]
\item[Q.] \AskItem{Do you believe that human beings were created in the image of God and possess a dignity bestowed by their Creator?}
\item[Q.] \AskItem{Have you come to recognize your need to know the One who created you and gave you life?}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\textbf{(2) No One Can Escape God's Righteous Judgment}

\begin{ScriptureBox}
\item[2:23] I am he who searches hearts and minds, and I will repay each of you according to your deeds.
\item[20:12--15] And I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and books were opened. Another book was opened, which is the book of life. The dead were judged according to what they had done, as recorded in the books. The sea gave up the dead that were in it, and death and Hades gave up the dead that were in them, and each person was judged according to what they had done. Then death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. The lake of fire is the second death. Anyone whose name was not found written in the book of life was thrown into the lake of fire.
\end{ScriptureBox}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, labelsep=0.3em]
\item[Q.] \AskItem{Do you believe that God sees beyond outward actions and searches the innermost thoughts and desires of the human heart?}
\item[Q.] \AskItem{Do you understand that every person must die and face God's judgment?}
\item[Q.] \AskItem{Sin is more than a handful of mistakes or a failure to meet a moral standard. At its heart, sin is turning away from God and refusing to live according to His will. It is choosing our own way, placing ourselves at the center of our lives instead of honoring the One who made us. Have you considered sin in this light?}
\item[Q.] \AskItem{A person may possess admirable qualities and may have done many good things. Yet none of these proves that we have never sinned against God, nor can our good deeds erase our guilt before Him. Have you recognized your need for forgiveness?}
\item[Q.] \AskItem{We often live as though we belong to ourselves, pursuing wealth, achievement, family, and personal fulfillment while neglecting our Creator. We may even give the gifts of creation the place that belongs to God alone. Have you considered whether anything in your life has taken His place?}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\textbf{(3) Jesus Christ, the Son of God, Cleanses Us from Sin by His Blood}

\begin{ScriptureBox}
\item[1:5] To him who loves us and has freed us from our sins by his blood.
\item[5:9] For you were slain, and with your blood you purchased for God persons from every tribe and language and people and nation.
\end{ScriptureBox}

Why do we need the blood of Christ? Because we have all sinned against the holy God. Sin is not a debt we can repay simply by doing good deeds, nor is our guilt something we can wash away through our own efforts.

Yet God has not abandoned sinners to remain in their sin. In His great love, He sent Jesus Christ to lay down His life for us. Through His death, Christ bore the penalty of sin and redeemed a people for God. Those who belong to Him are forgiven and cleansed, no longer standing before God on the strength of their own righteousness, but on the merits of the Savior who gave His life for them.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\textbf{(4) We All Need the Salvation Christ Alone Can Give}

\begin{ScriptureBox}
\item[7:14] They have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb.
\end{ScriptureBox}

Who are these people standing before God's throne? They are not people who have never sinned. They are those who have come through great tribulation and have been cleansed by the blood of the Lamb. Their purity is not the fruit of their own perfection, but the gift of Christ's redeeming sacrifice.

The blood of the Lamb, which might seem to speak of death and defeat, becomes the very means by which His people are cleansed and made ready to stand in God's presence. This is the wonder of the gospel: what we could never accomplish for ourselves, Christ has accomplished for us.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\textbf{(5) God Calls Us to Repent, Believe in Christ, and Freely Receive His Salvation}

\begin{ScriptureBox}
\item[22:17] The Spirit and the bride say, ``Come!'' And let the one who hears say, ``Come!'' Let the one who is thirsty come; and let the one who wishes take the free gift of the water of life.
\item[21:4] He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the old order of things has passed away.
\end{ScriptureBox}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, labelsep=0.3em]
\item[Q.] \AskItem{Will you respond to God's gracious invitation today and come to Him through Jesus Christ?}
\item[Q.] \AskItem{You do not need to make yourself perfect before you come to God. Come as you are, confess your sins, repent, and turn to Him. Believe that Jesus Christ died for sinners and rose again, receive the salvation He freely offers, and begin to follow Him in faith. Will you take this step and entrust your life to Him?}
\end{itemize}

The final vision of Revelation is not merely a warning of judgment or a glimpse of the end of the present world. It is the promise that God will dwell with His people, wipe away every tear, and make all things new. Death, sorrow, and pain will be no more. The invitation of the gospel is therefore both urgent and full of hope: come to Christ, the Lamb who was slain and who lives forever. The water of life is offered freely to all who come to Him.
